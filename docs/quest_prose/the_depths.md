# The Depths

## Slag

There is no recipe for slag. The Cupola rakes a lump into its fourth slot every eighth smelt.

Counted, not rolled. If that slot is full the count carries until there is room.

## Or: Concrete Powder

The Separator splits a lump into gray concrete powder, with scrap metal as the byproduct. Worth knowing before every lump goes to the portal.

## The Slag Furnace

Burns fuel, not power. Charcoal goes in the bottom slot; coal does not exist yet.

One lump takes twice as long as an ordinary smelt.

## The Only Obsidian

One lump, one block. Nothing else here makes obsidian, and each lump cost eight Cupola smelts.

A portal needs ten blocks, or fourteen if you fill the corners.

## Techno-Organic Waste

Right-click to pick through it, the same as a mound up top. The depths are solid from bedrock floor to bedrock ceiling, so this is most of what is down here.

It does not fall.

## Lignite

Brown coal, the rank below the coal vanilla ships.

It burns on its own at half a coal, so it is useful before you upgrade it.

## Ancient Sculk

Seams run through the fill, lit from inside.

A diamond sledgehammer, or netherite. Copper and iron leave nothing behind however long you swing. The diamond one wants a whole diamond block, so this is gem-tier work however early you find a seam.

## Nether Wart

Separate Rendered Organics for it. Wart grows on soul sand and nothing else, which the rubble already gives you.

## Slag Rubble

This one does fall. Sort it rather than stack it.

## Netherite Scrap

Out of the waste, uncommonly. There is no ancient debris to mine for, though a bastion chest may hold some.

## Terrain You Craft

The rock generates no vanilla nether block. Netherrack, basalt, blackstone and both nyliums come out of slag rubble as shards, four to a block.

Fortresses and bastions still generate and still hold what they always did, so you can find some there. You cannot mine for them.

## Soul Sand

Soul sand and soul soil arrive as Clumps, not shards. Search JEI for the clump.

## Nylium, and Fungus Forests

Bone meal on nylium grows a fungus. Bone meal on that fungus, standing on its own nylium, grows a huge one.

Nothing down here seeds a forest on its own, so these two shards are the only way to start one. A forest is renewable stems and hyphae.

## Coal, At Last

Smelt the lignite in the Burn Barrel. You do not need the Cupola.

One lignite spent as fuel smelts four more.

This is the only coal in the world.

## A Catalyst Needs a Shard

Three to five powder a block, never the block itself. One packs back into sculk, two make four veins.

Eight around redstone is a sensor, around soul sand a shrieker. Around an echo shard, the catalyst. The only shard in this world sits in a sewer sump, back up in the yard.

## A Blaze Rod, Without a Fortress

Press four blaze powder into a briquette at any bench, then fire the briquette in the Sintering Kiln.

Four powder in, and a rod breaks back into two, so do not round-trip them.

This is a brewing stand without a fortress.

## The Worn Forging Die

These come out of the waste about six times as often as netherite scrap.

Tear one down at the Workbench with a Prybar. It gives back almost nothing, but each one teaches a quarter of the netherite upgrade. Four and you have the pattern.

That blueprint is the only smithing template you can make here.
